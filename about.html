About me

Hello, I'm Elham.

I'm a PhD student in Educational Leadership at the University of Nevada, Reno, and I'm also completing an MS in Instructional Technology and Learning Design. I work as a Graduate Teaching Assistant in the Department of Educational Studies. Before coming to the U.S., I spent over a decade teaching English in Iran to learners from age 5 to 60.

Early in my career I partnered with a developer to build an EFL pronunciation program, and that experience shaped my interest in how technology can widen, or narrow, access to learning. Today I research AI in education, digital exclusion, and inclusive online classrooms.

Professional summary

Mixed-methods UX researcher and instructional designer focused on digital equity, inclusive design, and generative AI. I turn complex learning and technical challenges into clear user insights and accessible digital products.

I design and run research studies, including surveys, interviews, focus groups, ethnographies, and usability tests, and synthesize qualitative and quantitative data to guide design decisions. I enjoy building cross-functional partnerships and delivering data-driven recommendations that improve learning experiences and promote equitable access.
