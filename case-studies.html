AI in College Composition

How do undergraduate students use AI in writing courses, and what do they consider "ethical" use? A survey study capturing perspectives from beginner to advanced AI users.

Exploring:

  • How students use AI for brainstorming, drafting, and editing
  • How they define ethical AI use in academic writing
  • Perceived impact on learning, writing quality, and honesty
  • Comfort, trust, and willingness to learn AI tools
MethodSurvey
UsersUndergraduate writers

Inclusion in Online Courses

Online learning increases access, but it doesn't automatically make students feel connected. This study examines graduate students' sense of inclusion and belonging in asynchronous courses.

Exploring:

  • How course design and instructor presence shape belonging
  • The role of peer interaction without real-time contact
  • Challenges and positive practices students experience
  • Practical recommendations for course design
MethodZoom focus groups
UsersGraduate students
